# Design decisions

The boldness belongs to the city: original seeded Canvas buildings, three indigo depth layers, amber windows, a cyan suspension bridge and water reflections. Static skyline/bridge rasters are cached per resize; only lighting, traffic and reflections animate. The same canvas becomes the results comparison. No stock city asset, 3D package, dashboard template, or fake loading delays.

Bricolage Grotesque for identity/navigation and Source Serif 4 for reading, bundled locally. Ink #080f1c, indigo #17243d, amber #f5bb67, cyan #75dce5, ivory #e8e6df, muted #a3adbd. Text-token contrast exceeds 4.5:1 in the executable contrast check. Ruled sections, source rows and asymmetric editorial summaries avoid identical rounded cards. Reading passages stay under 80 characters per line.

The city supplies metaphor and emphasis—not the only accessible data channel. Named district buttons and a numeric heatmap expose the same scores, each opening exact source evidence. Scores describe the small edition sample, not national opinion or factual truth. Original-script quotations and limited confidence stay visible.

First-session intro is 5.5 seconds, skippable via click/key/button with guarded sessionStorage. Reduced motion is static; hidden tabs pause. Interactive parallax and broad blur filters were deliberately cut for performance. 60fps needs an actual laptop measurement; VM builds cannot prove it.

## Creative proposals
1. Missing Voices — built: ask whose context is absent from retrieved text, not imply censorship.
2. Claim Cross-check — built: shared/single/different-emphasis/disputed, with source IDs.
3. Evidence Lock — built: every nonzero theme needs an exact source quote and drill-down.
4. Headline De-escalator — partially covered by loaded-word neutral alternatives; no separate mode.
5. Framing Time-lapse — cut: historical sampling/data costs and sparse comparability.

The top three give the strongest trust/utility-to-effort ratio. Native audio controls and transcript replace a fake waveform/karaoke timer. Browser print/Save as PDF replaces a heavy report dependency. No stereotype-based persona toggle or ambient audio credit spending.
